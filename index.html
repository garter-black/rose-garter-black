A modern approach

A system designed around each person.

Most of us when we started working in the sex industry, were clueless and blind on which places to work at, and had to experiment trying multiple different places one by one, a very tedious and often time-wasting process not to mention some places are terribly run and some are quite dodgy. We do not wish other people to go through the same process. This is now the information age, and it’s our wish that new people starting can get personalized suggestions through us on which places in Sydney would be the best match. Rather than do things the typical way, we’ve created a system designed around each person’s needs.

Tailored suggestions

Providing you with tailored suggestions

Everyone is unique, and every brothel has a different customer base with statistically different preferences. Certain nationalities may be busy in one shop, but not busy in another shop. Certain body types are preferable in one shop, but not in another. Age, height, weight, cup size, nationality, background, all play a factor and Sydney having so many brothels may be overwhelming for someone new to try and research and choose which place to work with. We take all these factors and suggest you the places you have the highest chance to be the busiest.

Jobs in one shift

Most shifts landed between 3 and 7 jobs. One in ten reached 10 or more.

The range, day by day

Each line runs from the quietest woman's shift to the busiest that day; the dot is the middle. The whole shop did between 74 and 162 jobs a day, with 15 to 29 women on.

How many shifts reached each amount

Recorded pay is what the job sheets show. Tips are not recorded, so the with-tips figures are our estimate: tips usually make up about a fifth of what a woman takes home.

Our estimate

About 1 in 5 reach $2,000 with tips

Top shifts usually record around $1,500, and a $300 to $700 tip takes them to about $2,000. In this fortnight, 18% of all the women (and 25% of those who worked five or more days) had at least one shift that recorded $1,500 or more.

The ceiling, not the typical week

Best weeks

The best seven days in this sample recorded $10,230, about $12,800 with tips. The best single week we have seen anyone earn was $17,600. Plan your life around the typical numbers above, not the best ones.

Source: job sheets from previous shops we managed, 1–15 October 2018: 315 shifts by 72 women. Recorded pay excludes tips; tip figures are our estimate.
